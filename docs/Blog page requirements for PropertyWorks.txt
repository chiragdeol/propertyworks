PropertyWorks Knowledge Center Module
Project
PropertyWorks – Real Estate Intelligence & Advisory

1. Objective
The objective is to create a dedicated section called:
Knowledge Center
The Knowledge Center will serve as the educational and advisory content section of the PropertyWorks website.
The purpose is to:
Establish PropertyWorks as a trusted real estate advisory brand.
Improve Google SEO rankings.
Improve visibility on AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity.
Educate residential buyers, commercial buyers and investors.
Generate organic traffic.
Support lead generation for PropertyWorks Intelligence Reports.
The PropertyWorks team should be able to independently create and publish articles without requiring developer support.

2.. URL Structure
The Knowledge Center homepage should be:
https://www.propertyworks.in/knowledge-center
Each article should have an SEO-friendly URL.
Example:
https://www.propertyworks.in/knowledge-center/best-townships-in-thane
https://www.propertyworks.in/knowledge-center/raymond-ten-x-vs-lodha-amara
