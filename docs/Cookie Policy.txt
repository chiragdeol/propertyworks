2. What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer, smartphone, tablet, or other device when you visit a website.
Cookies help websites remember information about your visit, improve user experience, analyze website performance, and enable certain website functionalities.
Cookies do not typically contain information that directly identifies you, but they may be linked to personal information that you voluntarily provide through forms or enquiries.
